#### Clean up resources

#### Delete DynamoDB tables

1. Open the **DynamoDB** console at https://console.aws.amazon.com/dynamodb/.

2. In the navigation pane, select **Table**.

3. In the list of tables, select the tables related to the lab.

4. Select **Delete Table**. You are asked to confirm your selection.
